# YouthTempo

> Project Documentation v2.1 · 项目文档 v2.1

YouthTempo is a bilingual youth mental health support platform preparing for a small-scale school pilot. It helps young people understand emotions, build healthier everyday rhythms, and reach appropriate support before challenges become crises.

YouthTempo 是一个正在准备小规模学校试点的双语青少年心理健康支持平台。它帮助年轻人理解情绪、建立更健康的日常节律，并在困扰恶化为危机之前连接到合适的支持。

**Pilot focus:** young people aged 14–18, together with guardians, teachers, school leads, platform administrators, and verified professional supporters.

**试点重点：**14–18 岁青少年，以及与他们相关的家长、老师、学校负责人、平台管理员和经过验证的专业支持者。

[Visit the YouthTempo pilot site](https://youthtempo.com)

[访问 YouthTempo 试点网站](https://youthtempo.com)

## 1. Project Overview

YouthTempo brings self-reflection, everyday rhythm awareness, and human support pathways into one calm, accessible experience. Young people can complete a SWEET check-in, use guided tools to organize emotions and worries, review their history, and decide when to reach out. Authorized family and school participants receive role-appropriate views without replacing the young person's own voice.

YouthTempo 将自我梳理、日常节律觉察和现实求助路径放进一个平静、易用的体验中。年轻人可以完成 SWEET 日常记录，借助引导工具整理情绪与担忧，查看自己的历史变化，并判断何时需要向他人求助。获得授权的家庭和学校成员会看到与自身职责相符的信息，同时保留年轻人作为主体的表达空间。

The project is built for pilot learning rather than large-scale commercial claims. Its current purpose is to test usability, safety, data boundaries, and collaboration across young people, families, schools, and professional support.

项目当前服务于真实试点学习，而不是追求大规模商业化叙事。现阶段重点是验证产品是否好用、安全，数据边界是否清楚，以及青少年、家庭、学校与专业支持之间能否形成可靠协作。

## 2. Problem Background

Young people may notice that sleep, energy, appetite, movement, concentration, or emotions have changed, yet still find it difficult to explain what is happening or decide whom to approach. Support often arrives only after difficulties become highly visible.

年轻人可能已经感觉到睡眠、精力、饮食、运动、专注或情绪发生变化，却仍然很难说清楚自己正在经历什么，也不知道可以向谁开口。很多支持往往要等到问题变得十分明显之后才出现。

YouthTempo explores an earlier and lower-pressure entry point: notice everyday patterns, put experiences into words, take one manageable next step, and connect with trusted people when needed.

YouthTempo 尝试提供一个更早、更低压力的入口：先看见生活中的变化，把经历慢慢说清楚，找到一个当下做得到的小行动，并在需要时连接到可信任的人。

## 3. Vision & Mission

**Vision:** A support environment where young people can better understand themselves and seek help before difficulties escalate.

**愿景：**让年轻人在困难升级之前，就能更好地理解自己，并更自然地获得支持。

**Mission:** Combine an evidence-informed everyday rhythm framework, approachable digital tools, and clear human support pathways in a product that young people can use without feeling judged or medicalized.

**使命：**把基于证据启发的日常节律框架、容易使用的数字工具和清晰的现实求助路径结合起来，让年轻人在不被评判、不过度医疗化的环境中获得支持。

YouthTempo does not diagnose, treat, or cure mental health conditions. Its role is to support awareness, reflection, communication, and timely help-seeking.

YouthTempo 不诊断、治疗或治愈心理疾病。它的作用是支持觉察、梳理、表达和及时求助。

## 4. Target Users

- **Young people aged 14–18:** the primary school-pilot users, completing check-ins and using support tools.
- **Guardians:** viewing information for linked young people within confirmed relationships and consent boundaries.
- **Teachers and school support staff:** following assigned students, changes over time, and messages requiring attention.
- **School leads:** reviewing school-level participation and trends without making individual records the default view.
- **Platform administrators:** managing schools, safety operations, permissions, and audit workflows.
- **Verified professional supporters:** participating through a controlled verification and review process.
- **Young adults aged 18–25:** an additional independent-use pathway outside the core school pilot.

- **14–18 岁青少年：**学校试点的核心用户，完成日常记录并使用支持工具。
- **家长：**在已确认的关联关系和同意边界内，查看关联孩子的信息。
- **老师与学校支持人员：**关注本人负责的学生、阶段变化和需要了解的来信。
- **学校负责人：**查看学校层面的参与情况和趋势，而不是默认浏览每个学生的原始记录。
- **平台管理员：**管理学校、安全运营、权限和审计流程。
- **经过验证的专业支持者：**通过受控的资质提交和审核流程参与平台。
- **18–25 岁青年：**在学校试点之外，使用独立的成年人入口。

## 5. SWEET Model

SWEET is YouthTempo's evidence-informed product framework for reflecting on five everyday rhythms:

- **S — Sleep:** rest, bedtime, and sleep quality.
- **W — Wake:** getting up, daytime energy, and readiness to begin the day.
- **E — Eat:** regular meals and everyday nourishment.
- **E — Exercise:** movement and physical activity.
- **T — Task Engagement:** participation in study, responsibilities, and meaningful daily tasks.

SWEET 是 YouthTempo 用于日常梳理的循证启发型产品框架，关注五个与生活状态密切相关的节律维度：

- **S — Sleep：**休息、入睡和睡眠感受。
- **W — Wake：**起床、白天精力和开始一天的状态。
- **E — Eat：**规律饮食和日常营养。
- **E — Exercise：**身体活动与运动。
- **T — Task Engagement：**对学习、责任和有意义日常任务的投入。

The model helps users notice patterns and changes; it is not a diagnostic scale. A check-in produces a concise, non-diagnostic reflection and one manageable next step.

这个模型用于帮助用户觉察模式和变化，不是心理诊断量表。完成一次记录后，用户会得到简短、非诊断性的小结，以及一个更容易行动的下一步建议。

## 6. Product Features

### Check-in

A short SWEET-based reflection that helps users review the day, receive a structured summary, and save their history when eligible.

基于 SWEET 的简短日常梳理，帮助用户回顾当天状态、获得结构化小结，并在符合条件时保存历史记录。

### Mood Journal

Helps users name emotions, organize an experience, and find words for asking for support.

帮助用户辨认情绪、整理一段经历，并找到更自然的表达方式，以便在需要时向他人说明自己的感受和支持需求。

### Talk

A brief, supportive AI conversation for making one difficult situation feel clearer, with defined boundaries and urgent-support guidance.

通过简短、支持性的 AI 对话，帮助用户把眼前最困扰的一件事说清楚一点，同时保留明确的能力边界和紧急求助提示。

### Worry Time

A guided reflection tool for separating what can be acted on now from what can be set down temporarily.

通过引导式梳理，帮助用户区分现在能处理的事情和可以暂时放下的担忧。

### Referral

A practical pathway for deciding who to contact and when additional support may be appropriate.

帮助用户判断可以联系谁，以及在什么情况下需要进一步支持，把“去求助”转化为更清楚的行动路径。

### Account & Records

Email OTP authentication, personal SWEET history, profile management, consent status, data export, and account deletion controls.

提供 Email OTP 登录、个人 SWEET 历史、资料管理、同意状态、数据导出和账户注销入口。

### Messages

A permission-aware channel connecting young people with authorized adults, including anonymous-to-recipient options and safety review where needed.

为青少年与获得授权的成年人提供有权限边界的沟通渠道，并支持对收件人匿名及必要的安全复核。

### Community

A role-scoped space for supportive posts and comments, with configurable visibility, reporting, moderation, blocking, and restriction workflows.

一个按身份范围组织的支持性交流空间，提供可见范围选择、举报、审核、屏蔽和限制流程。

### School & Support Workspaces

Role-specific views for guardians, assigned teachers, school leads, platform administrators, and verified professional supporters.

为家长、负责老师、学校负责人、平台管理员和经过验证的专业支持者提供与职责相匹配的工作界面。

### Bilingual Experience

Chinese and English support across the public website, core authenticated experience, and AI support tools.

公开网站、核心登录后体验和 AI 支持工具均提供中文与英文支持。

## 7. AI Support Philosophy

YouthTempo uses AI as a supportive tool for reflection, organization, and communication. It can help summarize a check-in, offer one manageable next step, or help a user find words for an experience.

YouthTempo 将 AI 定位为辅助梳理、组织和表达的支持工具。它可以帮助总结一次日常记录、提供一个做得到的小行动，或协助用户找到描述经历的语言。

AI is not treated as a therapist, clinician, decision-maker, or substitute for human relationships. Responses avoid diagnosis and exaggerated reassurance, remain grounded in what the user has shared, and direct users toward real-world support when digital assistance is no longer appropriate.

AI 不被视为治疗师、临床人员、最终决策者，也不替代真实的人际支持。回应避免诊断和夸张承诺，只围绕用户实际表达的内容展开；当数字工具不再适合继续支持时，产品会引导用户转向现实中的帮助。

The AI layer supports both Chinese and English while preserving consistent response structures and product boundaries.

AI 层同时支持中文和英文，并在两种语言中保持一致的返回结构和产品边界。

## 8. Safety Architecture

YouthTempo follows a safety-first, layered approach:

1. Validate request method, input shape, and input size.
2. Apply deterministic bilingual safety checks before the relevant AI call.
3. Stop ordinary AI reflection when urgent support may be needed.
4. Route higher-risk messages and community content into human review workflows.
5. Apply authentication, consent, relationship, and role checks before protected actions.
6. Use rate limits, safe error responses, audit records, and operational monitoring as additional safeguards.

YouthTempo 采用安全优先的分层架构：

1. 先检查请求方式、输入结构和输入长度。
2. 在相关 AI 调用之前完成确定性的中英文安全识别。
3. 当用户可能需要紧急支持时，停止普通 AI 梳理流程。
4. 将风险较高的消息和社区内容送入人工复核流程。
5. 在受保护操作前验证身份、同意状态、关联关系和角色权限。
6. 通过频率限制、安全错误提示、审计记录和运行监控提供额外保护。

Safety detection supports timely escalation but does not claim perfect detection. Human review and real-world support remain essential.

安全识别用于帮助更及时地进入支持流程，但不声称能够做到百分之百准确。人工复核和现实支持仍然不可替代。

## 9. Privacy Protection

YouthTempo applies data minimization and role-appropriate access throughout the pilot. Users see only the information required for their role and confirmed relationships. Database-level Row Level Security, server-side authorization, consent records, and audit histories reinforce these boundaries.

YouthTempo 在试点中坚持数据最小化和按角色授权。用户只能看到完成自身职责所需、且符合已确认关系的信息；数据库行级安全、服务端权限判断、同意记录和审计历史共同强化这些边界。

The product includes user data export, account deletion, consent withdrawal, and school-exit handling. It does not promise absolute confidentiality or risk-free storage. Sensitive credentials and user content must never be committed to source control or exposed in client-side code.

产品提供用户数据导出、账户注销、同意撤回和学校退出后的数据处理流程，但不作“绝对保密”或“零风险存储”的承诺。敏感凭据和用户内容不得提交到源代码仓库，也不得暴露在客户端代码中。

An encrypted off-site backup and isolated recovery drill was completed on 11 August 2026. Daily encrypted backups on the Hong Kong server and off-site synchronization to a restricted maintainer device are enabled, and two consecutive runs have been verified. Recovery readiness remains conditional on cross-day observation, external failure alerts, and a real Email OTP/session check against the isolated recovery application.

2026 年 8 月 11 日已完成一次真实加密异地备份与隔离恢复演练。香港服务器的每日加密备份和到受限维护设备的异地同步均已启用，并已验证连续两次运行。恢复准备状态仍取决于跨日连续观察、外部失败告警，以及隔离恢复应用上的真实 Email OTP/session 验收。

## 10. Technical Architecture

```text
Browser UI
Pages · Views · Components · i18n
                    |
                    v
Next.js Pages Router and API Routes
          |                         |
          v                         v
Auth · Authorization · Consent   AI Safety Layer
          |                         |
          v                         v
Supabase Auth + PostgreSQL       AI Provider API
          |
          v
RLS · Role Protection · Relationships · Audit Records
```

The application uses the Next.js Pages Router. `pages/` defines browser routes and API endpoints, `views/` contains primary page implementations, `components/` contains shared UI, and `lib/` centralizes authentication, authorization, localization, safety, and product logic.

应用使用 Next.js Pages Router。`pages/` 定义浏览器路由和 API，`views/` 承载主要页面实现，`components/` 保存共享界面组件，`lib/` 集中维护身份认证、权限、国际化、安全和产品逻辑。

Supabase provides Email OTP authentication and PostgreSQL persistence. API routes authenticate callers and derive access from trusted database relationships instead of client-submitted roles. RLS and database triggers provide defense in depth.

Supabase 提供 Email OTP 身份认证和 PostgreSQL 数据存储。API 会验证调用者身份，并从可信数据库关系中判断访问范围，而不是信任客户端提交的角色；RLS 和数据库触发器提供进一步保护。

The production application is built as a standalone Docker image and deployed to Tencent Cloud Lighthouse in Hong Kong. Nginx terminates HTTPS and proxies requests to the application container bound to localhost. The canonical production URL is [`https://youthtempo.com`](https://youthtempo.com), and `www.youthtempo.com` redirects to the apex domain.

正式应用构建为 standalone Docker 镜像，并部署在腾讯云香港轻量应用服务器（Lighthouse）。Nginx 负责 HTTPS 与反向代理，应用容器仅绑定本机回环地址。正式地址为 [`https://youthtempo.com`](https://youthtempo.com)，`www.youthtempo.com` 会跳转到主域名。

### Production deployment automation / 正式环境自动部署

Every push to `main` first runs the repository's `Verify` workflow. A production deployment starts only after that workflow succeeds. GitHub Actions connects with a dedicated command-only SSH key, builds the exact verified commit on the Hong Kong server, starts a candidate container on localhost port 3001, and checks it before replacing the production container on port 3000. If the post-cutover health check fails, the deployment script restores the previous image.

每次推送到 `main` 后，仓库都会先执行 `Verify`。只有该工作流全部通过，正式部署才会开始。GitHub Actions 使用一把只能执行部署命令的专用 SSH 密钥，香港服务器会构建已经验证的准确 commit，先在本机 3001 端口启动候选容器并完成健康检查，再替换 3000 端口上的正式容器；如果切换后的检查失败，部署脚本会恢复上一镜像。

The production environment file and all private credentials remain on the server or in GitHub Actions Secrets; they are not stored in the repository. Re-running a deployment should be done by re-running the corresponding `Deploy Production` workflow rather than bypassing `Verify`.

正式环境文件和所有私密凭据只保存在服务器或 GitHub Actions Secrets 中，不进入仓库。需要重新部署时，应重新运行对应的 `Deploy Production` 工作流，不得绕过 `Verify`。

## 12. Internationalization

YouthTempo supports:

- Chinese `zh-CN` as the default language at `/`.
- English at `/en/`.
- A navigation language switcher without browser-language auto-redirect.
- Locale persistence through a cookie.
- Localized metadata, forms, accessibility text, core authenticated UI, and AI responses.

YouthTempo 支持：

- 中文 `zh-CN` 作为默认语言，对应 `/`。
- English 对应 `/en/`。
- 通过导航栏切换语言，不根据浏览器语言自动跳转。
- 使用 Cookie 保留用户的语言选择。
- 页面元信息、表单、无障碍文本、核心登录后界面和 AI 回应均支持双语。

Chinese is the product-copy source of truth, and the Chinese and English dictionaries share the same structured key shape. Internal roles, statuses, permission identifiers, timestamps, and user-generated content are not translated at the storage layer.

中文是产品文案的 source of truth，中英文词典保持相同的结构化 key。内部角色、状态、权限标识、时间数据和用户生成内容不会在存储层被翻译。

## 15. Roadmap

1. Close Email OTP delivery, recovery-operation, and external-alert conditions.
2. Complete signed policy, school-response, and physical-device acceptance.
3. Run a small, consent-based school pilot and collect role-specific feedback.
4. Review usability, safety operations, participation patterns, and data governance before expanding scope.
5. Improve infrastructure, documentation, and partner workflows based on pilot evidence.

YouthTempo 的路线图优先保障试点可用，而不是继续堆叠功能：

## 17. Security Notes

- Never commit API keys, service credentials, database connection strings, OTPs, sessions, or user data.
- Browser code must never receive service-level credentials.
- Profile roles and other permission-bearing fields remain server-controlled.
- Database schema, migrations, RLS, triggers, and production configuration require explicit review and approval.
- Do not bypass consent, relationship, moderation, or safety-review workflows.
- Do not translate or rewrite internal database roles, statuses, and permission identifiers.
- Do not log sensitive mental health content in operational reports or test output.
- AI features must remain non-diagnostic and must not claim to replace qualified professionals or emergency help.
- Recovery exercises must use an isolated, disposable environment and must never target production.

- 不得提交 API key、服务凭据、数据库连接信息、验证码、session 或用户数据。
- 浏览器代码不得获得服务端级别的凭据。
- 用户角色和其他承载权限的字段必须由服务端控制。
- 数据库 schema、migration、RLS、触发器和生产配置必须经过明确复核与授权。
- 不得绕过知情同意、关联关系、内容审核或安全复核流程。
- 不得翻译或改写数据库内部角色、状态和权限标识。
- 不得在运行报告或测试输出中记录敏感心理健康内容。
- AI 功能必须保持非诊断定位，不得声称替代专业人员或紧急援助。
- 恢复演练只能使用隔离、可丢弃的环境，绝不能指向正式数据库。
